about the KUKIRIN G4

The KuKirin G4 is a powerful electric scooter designed for people who enjoy electric transportation and off-road riding. It has a powerful motor, large tires, and a long-lasting battery.

The scooter has a 2000W motor, a 60V battery, and a maximum advertised speed of 70 km/h. Its battery capacity is 20Ah. The battery voltage is 60V, so the energy rating is 1200Wh. This is a small powerful scooter!
